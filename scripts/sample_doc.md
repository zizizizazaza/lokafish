# Taylor Swift Eras Tour — Singapore Economic Brief

## Overview
Taylor Swift's Eras Tour will perform six sold-out shows at the National Stadium
in Singapore from 2 to 9 March 2024, with an exclusive-region deal that blocks
other Southeast Asian cities from hosting the tour. The Singapore Tourism Board
(STB) and Kallang Alive Sport Management partnered to secure the run.

## Stakeholders
- **Singapore Tourism Board (STB)** — coordinator of the Kallang Alive deal,
  responsible for marketing the event regionally and tracking visitor metrics.
- **Marina Bay Sands (MBS)** — flagship hotel for premium attendees, deploying
  surge pricing and concert-themed packages.
- **Singapore Airlines (SIA) and Changi Airport** — adding 240 supplementary
  flights on regional routes; expecting +20% inbound passenger movement.
- **Grab Holdings** — deploying additional drivers and shuttle services around
  Kallang during showtimes.
- **F&B operators** in Clarke Quay, Telok Ayer, Boat Quay, and around the
  stadium are extending hours and launching themed menus.
- **Local Swifties** in Singapore plus large inbound contingents from Malaysia,
  Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Australia, India, China, Japan, and Korea.

## Direct economic dimensions
- 6 nights × 55,000 capacity = ~330,000 ticketed attendees, of which industry
  estimates suggest 70% are international visitors.
- Hotel ADR in the Marina Bay corridor is reported at S$358.91 (+12.7% YoY)
  with peak occupancy of 92.7% on opening night.
- Tourism receipts during the concert week are projected to fall in the range
  S$300–500M depending on retention assumptions.
- Sector-multiplier estimates from MAS suggest mega-event activity carries a
  1.4–1.8x multiplier for the local economy.

## Long-tail dimensions
- Social-media exposure is expected to drive an indirect tourism uplift over
  the following 12 months. Key channels: TikTok, Instagram, X (Twitter),
  YouTube concert vlogs.
- The brand-equity boost for Singapore-as-destination is non-trivial; STB
  treats the run as a marketing investment beyond direct revenue.

## Risks and uncertainties
- Scalper margins leak revenue out of the local economy.
- Retail and F&B operators may capture less than the headline projection if
  international fans concentrate spend on accommodation only.
- The Kallang Alive exclusivity premium reduces the Treasury's net fiscal
  capture.
- Definitions of "viral coefficient" vary between social-media analytics and
  retail foot-traffic measurement, leading to disputes between forecasters.
