# Loka — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Financial researchers, economic analysts, and quantitative strategists who need to model
complex real-world scenarios and trust the outputs enough to act on them. They're
technically literate and data-fluent — they'll notice if something feels generic or
dumbed-down. They come in wanting answers, not decoration.

### Brand Personality
**Innovative · Striking · Intelligent**

Loka is the first of its kind — it should feel that way. Not a dashboard repackaged.
Not another SaaS product. Something genuinely new. The visual language should trigger
a "how does this even exist?" reaction while still projecting the calm authority needed
to trust a financial prediction tool.

Tone: confident without being loud. Precise without being sterile. The interface is
the proof that the technology is serious.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Striking, not decorative.** Visual weight is earned through structure and contrast,
  not gradients and shadows. Every bold choice should reinforce the sense of precision.
- **Light mode only** (current phase). Warm whites (#F7F6F3, #FFFFFF) as the base.
  Accent with deep blues (#2383E2) and high-contrast darks for punch.
- **Typographic hierarchy** does the heavy lifting — size, weight, and tracking over
  color washes. Inter for prose, JetBrains Mono for data/metrics.
- **Iconography**: stroke-only SVG (1.5px weight, rounded caps/joins, 24×24 viewBox).
  No emoji. No filled icons. No decorative illustrations.
- **Motion**: purposeful micro-interactions (counters, fade-ups, progress states).
  Nothing loops for the sake of motion. Animation is information.

### Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (too safe, too blue-gray)
- Over-illustrated fintech (too playful, breaks authority)
- Heavy dark-mode "hacker" aesthetics (not the target for this phase)
- Notion clones that don't push the visual language further

### Design Principles
1. **First impression = credibility.** A researcher trusts the tool before they trust
   the data. Visual precision signals methodological precision.
2. **Data is the hero.** Typography, spacing, and layout exist to make numbers,
   charts, and model outputs maximally scannable and legible.
3. **Restraint enables impact.** When everything is bold, nothing is. Use strong
   visual moments sparingly — on the hero, on key metrics, on decision moments.
4. **Innovative ≠ experimental.** New patterns should feel inevitable, not jarring.
   Push the visual language forward without losing orientation.
5. **Every empty state is an opportunity.** Loaders, blank slates, and transition
   states should reinforce confidence in the system, not just fill space.
